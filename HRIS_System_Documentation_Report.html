Section 2

HRIS Overview

HRIS Manila Council is a web application used by the City Council of Manila's Human Resource Management (HRM) Section to maintain 201-file records, process leave applications, clearances, and other HR transactions for the Council's employees, and to give employees self-service access to their own records.

2.1 Who uses it

User groupWhat they use HRIS for
HRM Section (HR/Admin)Maintains every employee's 201 file, processes leave/clearance, configures system-wide lookups, generates reports.
All Council employeesSelf-service: view/edit own PDS, file leave, view own clearance/service record/leave card, change password.
Immediate SupervisorsEndorse, deny, or return-to-HR leave applications awaiting endorsement.
Secretary to the City CouncilCouncil Review stage of every documents-required leave application.
Vice-Mayor / Presiding OfficerFinal approval, denial, or return-to-HR of leave applications of 15 or more working days.

2.2 Technology stack

Language / runtimeJava 11
FrameworkSpring Boot 2.2.4 (Web, Data JPA, Data REST, Security, Thymeleaf)
PackagingWAR with embedded Tomcat, deployed at target/hrisp.war
DatabaseMySQL — schema hris_01
ReportingJasperReports — .jrxml source compiled to .jasper at build time
FrontendServer-rendered Thymeleaf views, Bootstrap/Limitless admin theme, Select2 searchable combos, DataTables
Deploymentnginx reverse proxy (Let's Encrypt TLS) → Spring Boot on port 8082, managed by systemd (hrisp.service, auto-restart on crash)

2.3 Current scale

Live domainmanilacitycouncil.com
Active employee 201-file records625 hris_01.employee, verified 22-Jul-2026
Account roles6 — ADMIN, HR, EMPLOYEE, SUPERVISOR, COUNCIL, VICEMAYOR
Functional modules15 (Section 3)
Change Requests delivered to dateCR-015 through CR-023, continuously deployed
How the system has grown. HRIS began as a 201-file / PDS system (Employee Records, PDS, Appointments, Clearance, Service Record, 201 Files, System Settings). Leave Management (CR-016) was added as a full module with its own multi-stage decision workflow, later given real login accounts per approving actor (CR-016 revision 2) and further refined for routing and return-to-HR behavior (CR-020). Archive gained admin-manageable sub-folders today (CR-023). Every change is additive — the underlying 201-file core has not been rebuilt.

Section 4

Feature Summary per Module

What each module does today. Modules are grouped as in Section 3.

4.1 Employee Records EmployeeController

  • Create/edit the employee master record: name, birth details, gender, civil status, blood type, citizenship, government ID numbers (GSIS/BIR/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG), addresses, division/office/position, appointment & employee status.
  • Photo upload, login credential provisioning, and role assignment (Employee List → credentials).
  • Generates the per-employee empHashCode capability token used to gate every self-service (/my-*) URL.
  • Employee List, Clearance List and Appointments List are the primary admin search/browse screens.

4.2 Personal Data Sheet (PDS, CS Form 212)

  • Ten sub-sections: Personal Information, Family Background, Educational Background, Civil Service Eligibility, Work Experience (with a part-time flag), Voluntary Work, Learning & Development (with an hours-display override), Other Information (with configurable Other-Info Questions), References, Government IDs (with GSIS ID and date-mask validation).
  • Every section is editable by HR/Admin and, on the employee's own record, by the employee — enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI.
  • Long dropdowns are searchable Select2 combos; Office Name (Work Experience) and City (addresses) accept free-typed values.
  • Prints to the CS Form 212 PDF; empty fields print "N/A" rather than blank.

4.3 Appointments AppointmentController

  • HR records appointment papers per employee with document attachments.
  • Employees view their own via My Appointments.

4.4 Service Record ServiceRecordController

  • HR maintains position/office/salary/period history and manages the signatories printed on the form.
  • Prints the CSC Service Record form; employees view their own via My Service Record.

4.5 201 Files Docs201Controller

  • General per-employee document uploads, categorized by a configurable Document Type lookup.

4.6 Archive ArchiveController, ArchiveSection (CR-023)

  • HR/Admin-only file repositories, organized into folders: SALN, Resigned Employees, and Leaves out of the box.
  • As of today (CR-023): folders are admin-manageable — a "Manage Folders" screen lets an admin add, rename, or delete folders. A folder's code is immutable once created; deletion is blocked while files remain in it.
  • Per-folder customizable labels for grouping files, free-text tags, search, and client-side CSV/XLS export.

4.7 Leave Management LeaveController, LeaveWorkflowController

  • Employees file a CS Form 6 leave application (My Leave Record); HR screens, and the request proceeds through a multi-stage decision flow with dedicated actor accounts — Supervisor, Secretary to the City Council, and Vice-Mayor (full detail in Sections 5 and 6).
  • Employee's Leave Card ledger tracks running VL/SL balances; approved leave posts a deduction only for Vacation and Mandatory/Forced Leave (VL) or Sick Leave (SL) — other leave types are recorded without changing balances.
  • Monthly accrual (1.25 VL + 1.25 SL) is a manual HR action per employee; a scheduled/on-demand Year-End run deducts unused mandatory leave (Section 4.15).
  • Employees may appeal a disapproved application or cancel a still-pending one from My Leave Record.
  • Leave Tracker shows a calendar of approved leaves. Exports: CS Form 6 PDF, Leave Card PDF, and a Leave Verification Receipt PDF for leaves requiring supporting documents.

4.8 Clearance ClearanceController, ApproversController

  • Employees file a clearance application from My Clearance; each configured approver records a sign-off.
  • HR/Admin process outstanding clearances from the Clearance List and maintain the approver/signatory settings.
  • Approved or disapproved (with reason) clearance prints to PDF.

4.9 Training & Seminar TrainingSeminarController

  • HR records trainings/seminars per employee: title, provider, hours, dates, and an uploaded certificate.
  • Feeds the PDS Learning & Development section.

4.10 Notifications NotificationController

  • In-app bell notification on every account, with an unread badge that clears on open.
  • Notifications are session-scoped — a user only ever sees their own. Today's producer is the Leave decision flow (employee, per-stage actor, and HR alerts).

4.11 Dashboard & Navigation NavController

  • Routes each role to its working landing page on login: Dashboard for ADMIN/HR/EMPLOYEE, Leave Approvals for SUPERVISOR/COUNCIL/VICEMAYOR.
  • Quick-navigation badges surface pending work, e.g. the Leave Applications "New" count.

4.12 Auth, Roles & Change Password SecurityConfig, ChangePasswordController

  • Form login; passwords stored BCrypt, with legacy plain-text passwords auto-migrated on next successful login.
  • Six roles by userType: ADMIN, HR, EMPLOYEE, SUPERVISOR, COUNCIL, VICEMAYOR — assigned from Employee List → credentials.
  • Self-service password change at /change-password.

4.13 System Settings ~22 lookup controllers

  • CRUD reference-data screens: Departments, Divisions, Offices, Districts, Position Titles (+ Position Description Forms), Salary Grades, Levels, Employee Status, Appointment Status, Civil Status, Degree Courses/Levels, Academic Honors, Schools, Scholarships, Professions, Eligibilities, Document Types, Learning Types, Leave Types, Holidays, Other Info Questions, and the Leave Endorsement/Signatory defaults.
  • Employee Status drives the year-end coterminous exclusion (any status whose name contains "COTERM").
  • Leave signatories hold the default names printed on CS Form 6; every workflow action can still override them for an acting official.

4.14 Reports & PDF Exports ReportsController, jasper/reports/*.jrxml

  • Jasper-based PDFs: PDS (CS Form 212), CS Form 6 (leave application), Leave Card, Leave Verification Receipt, Clearance, Service Record, Position Description Form, Report on Separation, and individual Employee Reports.

4.15 Year-End Processing LeaveYearEndProcessor

  • Every December 31 at 23:30 (Asia/Manila), and on demand from Leave Applications → Year-End Mandatory/Forced Leave Processing, deducts each eligible employee's unused mandatory leave (5 minus days already used).
  • Coterminous employees are excluded and reported in the run summary; the run is idempotent per year.

End-to-End Process Flow — Every Module

Each module's flow expressed as User → System → Database → Output.

5.1 Employee Records

UserHR/Admin creates or edits an employee record
SystemEmployeeController validates, assigns empHashCode on create
Databaseemployee table (+ FK targets: division, office, position, employee_status)
OutputUpdated Employee List; record becomes the anchor for every other module

5.2 Personal Data Sheet

UserEmployee or HR edits a PDS section
SystemOwn-record-or-admin authorization check, then save
DatabasePer-section PDS tables (family_background, educational_background, work_experience, …)
OutputSaved to the 201 file; prints as the CS Form 212 PDF

5.3 Appointments

UserHR records an appointment, attaches documents
SystemAppointmentController saves record + file upload via StorageService
Databaseappointment table + stored file reference
OutputAppointments List (HR); My Appointments (employee, read-only)

5.4 Service Record

UserHR adds/edits a service-record period
SystemServiceRecordController saves entry, signatory settings applied
Databaseservice_record table
OutputCSC Service Record PDF; My Service Record (employee, read-only)

5.5 201 Files

UserHR uploads a document, tags it with a Document Type
SystemDocs201Controller stores the file via StorageService
Databasedocs_201 table + stored file reference
Output201 Files list for the employee

5.6 Archive

UserAdmin creates/selects a folder, uploads a file, optionally labels/tags it
SystemArchiveController validates the folder against archive_section, stores the file
Databasearchive_section, archive_file, archive_label
OutputFolder's file list; CSV/XLS export; sidebar reflects folders live (@ModelAttribute advice)
File leave (Employee)→ HR screening→ Supervisor endorsement→ HR / Council / Vice-Mayor review chain→ Leave Card posting→ PDF export

5.7 Leave Management (happy path — full branching in Section 6)

UserEmployee files; HR/Supervisor/Council/Vice-Mayor act at their stage
SystemLeaveWorkflowController enforces per-stage, per-role authorization; notifies next actor
Databaseleave_application, leave_workflow_action, leave_card_entry, notification
OutputLeave Card balance updated; CS Form 6 PDF, Leave Verification Receipt, Leave Card PDF

5.8 Clearance

UserEmployee files; each configured approver signs off
SystemClearanceController records each approver decision
Databaseclearance, per-approver sign-off rows
OutputApproved/Disapproved clearance PDF

5.9 Training & Seminar

UserHR records a training with a certificate upload
SystemTrainingSeminarController saves the record
Databasetraining_seminar table
OutputPDS Learning & Development section reflects the training

5.10 Notifications

UserAny module event (e.g. a leave stage transition)
SystemNotifier writes a notification for the affected user/role
Databasenotification table
OutputNavbar bell badge; marked read on open

5.11 System Settings

UserAdmin adds/edits/deletes a lookup value (e.g. a Leave Type)
SystemPer-lookup controller CRUD, most with a first-boot seeder
Database~22 dedicated lookup tables
OutputValue available in every dropdown/select that references it

5.12 Reports & PDF Exports

UserUser clicks an export/print action anywhere in the app
SystemController builds a parameter map, fills the matching .jasper template
DatabaseSource record(s) for the report being printed
OutputRendered PDF streamed to the browser

5.13 Year-End Processing

UserScheduler (Dec 31, 23:30) or HR clicks the on-demand run
SystemLeaveYearEndProcessor computes 5-minus-used per eligible employee, skips already-processed/coterminous
DatabaseADJUSTMENT row appended to leave_card_entry, period YE-<year>
OutputUpdated leave balances; run summary (processed / skipped / coterminous)

Module Interactions & Dependencies

No module is fully isolated — the Employee Record is the hub every other module reads from.

ModuleDepends on / feeds
Personal Data SheetReads Employee Record; Learning & Development section is fed by Training & Seminar entries.
Leave ManagementReads Employee Record + Employee Status (drives year-end coterminous exclusion); posts to its own Leave Card ledger; feeds Notifications; feeds Archive (Leaves folder).
ArchiveIndependent storage keyed by folder; receives documents originating from Leave (Verification Receipts) and HR-driven SALN / Resigned-employee filings.
ClearanceReads Employee Record; Approvers/signatory settings come from System Settings.
Appointments, Service Record, 201 FilesAll read the Employee Record; independent of each other.
Training & SeminarReads Employee Record; writes into the PDS Learning & Development view.
NotificationsWritten by the Leave decision flow today; read by every logged-in user's navbar.
System SettingsUpstream of nearly every module — Employee Status, Departments/Divisions/Offices, Position Titles, Leave Types, Holidays, Document Types, etc. are all referenced elsewhere.
Reports & PDF ExportsDownstream of every module — pulls live data at export time; never a system of record itself.
Year-End ProcessingReads Leave Management + Employee Status; writes back into the Leave Card ledger.
Employee Record (hub)→ PDS / Appointments / Service Record / 201 Files / Clearance / Leave→ Reports & PDF Exports
System Settings (lookups)→ Every module's dropdowns & business rules
Training & Seminar→ PDS Learning & Development
Leave Management→ Notifications→ Archive (receipts/records)

User Roles & Responsibilities

RoleResponsibilitiesLanding page
ROLE_ADMINFull system administration — every module, every System Settings screen, account/role provisioning.Dashboard
ROLE_HRHR records management, leave screening and every workflow stage as an acting-official fallback, clearance/appointment/service-record maintenance, Archive.Dashboard

Per-role authorization on Leave. A dedicated actor account may act only while an application sits at its own stage — Supervisor at FOR_ENDORSEMENT, Council at FOR_COUNCIL_REVIEW, Vice-Mayor at FOR_FINAL_APPROVAL. ROLE_HR/ROLE_ADMIN may act at every stage. Employees can never call a workflow action directly. Admins assign roles and reset passwords from Employee List → credentials.

9.2 Assumptions

  • "Complete HRIS Modules" groups functionality by controller/feature area, not by menu structure — some modules share a single navigation entry (e.g. System Settings covers ~22 screens).
  • Diagrams show the designed flow; edge cases (e.g. HR acting on behalf of an unavailable actor) are called out in prose where they materially change the picture.
  • This report does not include a security/vulnerability assessment — see the separate HRIS_Old_vs_New_Comparison_Report (13 July 2026) for that analysis.
