Section 5

Added Module: Leave Management

Workflow

  • Record CS Form 6 application
  • Auto-certify VL/SL credits
  • Recommend → Approve / Disapprove (exigency = no deduction)
  • Post to Leave Card ledger
  • Export Form 6 & Leave Card PDF

Data model 2026-leave-management.sql

  • leave_application — Form 6
  • leave_card_entry — ledger
  • leave_type · holiday — settings
  • FKs → employee
Ledger: monthly accrual 1.25 VL + 1.25 SL; only "V"/"S" entries deduct; special leaves recorded without changing balances. Employees get a read-only /my-leaves view.

Section 6

Setup & Security Parameters

Setup

  • JDK 11 · Maven → target/hrisp.war
  • MySQL hris_01
  • Profiles: SPRING_PROFILES_ACTIVE (dev/prod)
  • Secrets: DB_USERNAME/PASSWORD, EMPLOYEE_DEFAULT_PASSWORD (env, not committed)

Security

  • Form login · MigratingPasswordEncoder (BCrypt)
  • Roles: ROLE_ADMIN / HR / EMPLOYEE
  • empHashCode capability token on self-service
  • CSRF on (leave POSTs protected)
  • Upload allow-list + 5 MB cap

Migration order: back up DB → apply 2026-leave-management.sql + PDS migrations (additive) → deploy WAR → restart.
